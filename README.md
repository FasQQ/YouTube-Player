## What is this?

YouTube Player by FasQ is a lightweight wrapper around YouTube and YouTube Music that
turns them into a proper Windows application — with a taskbar icon, a system tray,
media key support on your keyboard, and a Discord status. You sign in with your
regular Google account and use everything you already know — just in a more
convenient form.

## ✨ Features

- 🏠 **Start screen** — choose whether you want regular YouTube or YouTube Music
- 🎛️ **Media keys** — play/pause/seek from your keyboard, even when the window isn't focused
- 📌 **System tray** — closing the window hides the app instead of quitting it
- 🖼️ **"Thumbnail only" mode** — on regular YouTube, hide the video and keep just the audio
- 🎮 **Discord status** — friends can see what you're listening to/watching, Spotify-style
- 🎛️ **Control from Discord** — an optional companion plugin (Vencord/BetterDiscord) adds a small panel inside Discord to see what's playing and control playback (play/pause, next/previous, shuffle, repeat) without switching windows
- 🌍 **Two languages** — Polish and English
- ⚙️ **Launch with Windows** — optional
- ⏫ **Automatic updates** — the app updates itself
- 🎵 **Music keeps playing in the background** — opening Settings or the start screen doesn't interrupt playback

## 📥 Installation

1. Go to the [releases page](https://github.com/FasQQ/YouTube-Player/releases/latest)
2. Download the `.exe` file (installer)
3. Run it and follow the instructions

> Windows may show a SmartScreen warning (because the installer isn't code-signed) —
> click **"More info" → "Run anyway"**.
